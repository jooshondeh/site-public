Know what is covered

Coverage Questions

The right plan starts with a shared understanding of services, costs, and responsibilities.

Talk through your requirements
Does monitoring include around-the-clock technician support?

Monitoring tools operate continuously for enrolled systems. Technician review, escalation, and response follow the support hours and commitments in your agreement. Critical after-hours response is available only when specifically contracted.

What is included in backup management?

We maintain the agreed backup configuration, review job status, follow up on failed jobs, and perform the sample restoration tests included in your plan. Backup subscriptions, storage, and major recovery work are separately quoted unless expressly included. Test scope and frequency are tailored to the systems and data in your agreement.

Who owns the software or backup subscription?

Your quote and agreement identify the subscription holder, licensing and storage costs, NGB’s administration responsibilities, and access arrangements. The purchasing arrangement can vary by product and provider.

Can you support equipment we already own?

Yes. Plans can cover approved existing equipment as well as equipment sourced through NexGen Binary. During onboarding, we review compatibility, condition, and support status, and identify any necessary upgrades or replacements.

What does vendor coordination mean?

We help gather troubleshooting details, work with your dental software or equipment provider, coordinate access, and follow up on the issue. The provider remains responsible for its product, licensing, and any services it supplies directly.

How do warranties and loaners work?

Warranty coordination helps you work with a manufacturer on eligible covered equipment. Parts, replacement equipment, shipping, manufacturer charges, and extensive repair work are separate unless agreed otherwise. Temporary loaners depend on availability, compatibility, and the terms of your agreement; physical servers require a separate arrangement.
